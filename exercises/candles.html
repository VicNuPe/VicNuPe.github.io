Our classic pillar candles are poured by hand into elegant, slow-burning shapes that suit any room in your home.

They make a beautiful centrepiece for your table and a thoughtful, ready-to-give present for someone special.

Our tea lights bring gentle warmth and soft fragrance to smaller spaces, perfect for bedrooms, bathrooms, and cosy evenings.

Every set is hand-poured in small batches, so you can mix and match your favourite scents to create your own glow.
