Ten titles that reshaped how we play, feel, and connect — the most influential video games of the past decade.

Explore the List Why These Games?

Video games don't just entertain — they build worlds, spark movements, and permanently alter culture. The past decade delivered a wave of titles that broke sales records, sparked global conversation, and pushed the medium forward as an art form. Here are the games that mattered most.

RPG  ·  2015

The Witcher 3:
Wild Hunt

CD Projekt Red's masterpiece set the gold standard for open-world RPGs. Its hand-crafted quests, morally complex choices, and sprawling world of Velen redefined what players expected from a story-driven game — with over 100 hours of content and two award-winning expansions.

200+ Game of the Year Awards  ·  50M+ copies sold

2015
Action-Adventure  ·  2017

Zelda: Breath
of the Wild

Nintendo shattered every open-world convention when Breath of the Wild launched alongside the Switch in 2017. Climb any surface, experiment with physics, solve puzzles in dozens of ways — its emergent design philosophy influenced every open-world game that followed.

Game of the Year 2017  ·  32M+ copies sold

2017
Battle Royale  ·  2017

Fortnite

Epic Games didn't invent the battle-royale genre, but Fortnite became a cultural juggernaut that transcended gaming entirely. Free-to-play, rapid season updates, and in-game concerts attracted 350 million+ players — the first game to host live virtual events at the scale of real-world concerts.

350M+ registered players  ·  $9B+ revenue in peak years

2017
Action-Adventure  ·  2018

Red Dead
Redemption 2

Rockstar Games delivered the most cinematic open-world narrative ever made. Arthur Morgan's story of loyalty, survival, and redemption resonated deeply. Its unprecedented attention to detail — animals with realistic behaviour, NPCs with dynamic routines — set a bar the industry is still chasing.

175+ Game of the Year Awards  ·  61M+ copies sold

2018
